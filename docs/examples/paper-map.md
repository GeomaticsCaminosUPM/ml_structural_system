# Paper results map

!!! warning "Altered footprints"
    Until the peer-reviewed version of the paper is published, the footprints without a survey label on this map are not the real ones. Their geometry, height and other attributes have been altered, so they look similar to the buildings of the area but do not match them. The surveyed buildings, which make up the training, validation and test sets, are real.

The buildings of the paper's six surveys in Guatemala City, San José and Santo Domingo, with the structural system predicted for every footprint by each city's models. Surveyed buildings are outlined. The map can also color the buildings by their surveyed class, by any model attribute, or by a model's test predictions, errors and agreement. The test scores and confusion matrix of the selected experiment and model are in the side panel.
